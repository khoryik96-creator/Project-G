# Project G — Active Story Canon

This file records approved story rules that override older proposal language where they conflict.

## Core Leads

### Xu Kai

- Publicly appears to be a physically unimpressive civilian strategist.
- Solves problems through observation, psychology, manipulation, economics, social engineering, bluffing, timing, and unhinged but logical schemes.
- Secretly possesses overwhelming martial ability, but this is **not to be indicated early**.
- Early chapters must not contain reader-only martial hints, impossible reflexes, mysterious weapon breaks, hidden pressure, suspicious knockouts, or similar winks.
- Readers should initially have a fair reason to believe Xu Kai genuinely relies on his mind because he lacks martial strength.
- His eventual martial reveal should therefore recontextualize the entire story rather than confirm an obvious early suspicion.
- He calls Jian Yue **“Oi.”**

### Jian Yue

- National Tier #2 and one of the country's Five Pillars.
- Proud, sharp-tongued, highly competent, and tsundere.
- Assigned to supervise Xu Kai after the Qingshi incident.
- Genuinely believes Xu Kai needs physical protection.
- Her repeated explanation for protecting, following, feeding, checking on, or otherwise caring about Xu Kai is that he is a **state security concern**.
- “Oi” begins as something she hates and gradually becomes something only Xu Kai is allowed to call her.

## Relationship Tone

- Dialogue-heavy.
- Banter before overt romance.
- Jian Yue's affection should grow through behavior, protectiveness, jealousy, and increasingly weak excuses rather than immediate confession.
- Xu Kai should tease her reactions without becoming cruel.
- Jian Yue remains genuinely formidable and important; romance never reduces her to a damsel.

## The Five Pillars

- All five possess National-Tier strength.
- They are national protectors, not conventional villains.
- Their conflict with Xu Kai comes from legitimate security concerns, incompatible methods, incomplete information, and personality clashes.
- **#1 Han Zhen** — Grand Marshal; strongest officially recognized martial artist in the country; disciplined, severe, military-minded, and initially deeply distrustful of Xu Kai.
- **#2 Jian Yue** — Sword specialist and female lead; Xu Kai's assigned supervisor.
- **#3** — currently deployed at the eastern frontier; full profile remains open. Chapter 14 ends with Pillar #3 demanding to meet Xu Kai.
- **#4 Lu Chen** — intelligence/security specialist; dry, observant, and increasingly resigned to Xu Kai's methods.
- **#5 Bai Ren** — elderly independent Pillar with a relaxed temperament and a sense of humor; introduced during the Hall of Five meeting.

## Current Political Arc

- A network informally referred to as **Blue Lantern** has been tracking the Five Pillars and measuring national response times when they are absent.
- Blue Lantern is not a conventional villain organization. Its members believe overdependence on the Five has weakened provincial/local institutions.
- Their original demonstrations were designed to expose that weakness without causing mass casualties.
- Minister Cao exploited the network and attempted to turn a controlled White Bridge demonstration into a lethal incident so provincial reformers would appear to be terrorists.
- The White Bridge plot was stopped without revealing any martial ability from Xu Kai.

## Power Reveal Rule

**Do not reveal or imply Xu Kai's true martial power in the early story.**

When the first undeniable reveal eventually happens, it must be a major story event with emotional stakes. Until then, every dangerous situation involving Xu Kai should be resolved through his wit, another character's strength, planning, environmental exploitation, social leverage, or believable luck that does not imply supernatural ability.
